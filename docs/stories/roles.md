# MeshWatch – User Roles

## Homeowner
Monitors environmental conditions in different rooms to maintain a comfortable living environment.

## Facility Manager
Monitors environmental conditions in buildings to ensure suitable indoor conditions.

## Farmer
Monitors temperature, humidity and light conditions to ensure suitable growing conditions for crops.

## Environmental Researcher
Collects and analyzes environmental measurements from multiple IoT nodes to study environmental conditions and identify trends and patterns.

## Service Control Centre Operator (e.g. ANSP)
Monitors environmental conditions in technical equipment and server rooms to identify abnormal conditions and support the availability of critical infrastructure.

## System Administrator
Manages the MeshWatch platform and monitors system health.

## Maintenance Technician
Identifies device failures and performs maintenance to keep the system running.
